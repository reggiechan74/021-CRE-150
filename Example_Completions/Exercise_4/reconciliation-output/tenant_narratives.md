## Unit 101 — CanadaFirst Bank (Net with CAM Cap)

Your FY2025 final charge is **$95,616.41** against pre-bills of $57,690.00,
producing a balance owing of **$37,926.41**.

Your lease places a 4%-compounded annual cap on **controllable** Operating
Expenses over the 2022 Base Year of $11.40 PSF, with realty tax, insurance,
utilities, and snow removal flowing through uncapped (**§6.05 / §6.05.1**).
The FY2025 controllable ceiling works out to **$12.82 PSF / $57,690 total**.

- **Controllable share:** $33,399.65 — well below the $57,690 ceiling, so the
  cap did **not** bind and no landlord absorption was required.
- **Uncontrollable pass-through:** $62,216.76 — driven primarily by the FY2025
  realty tax overage, which the lease expressly excludes from the cap.

The bulk of the true-up therefore reflects realty tax flowing through under
§6.05.1, not a controllable-cost overage.
