## Executive Summary

This is a **5-year, absolutely net (carry-all) multi-tenant industrial lease** for **Unit 1, 1234 Meyerside Drive, Mississauga, Ontario L5T 1A1** (~100,000 sq ft) between **2468193 Ontario Inc.** (Landlord) and **Acme Distribution Corp.** (Tenant). Both are Ontario corporations.

- **Term:** 5 years, 0 months — **Commencement Date 2026-06-01** through **Expiry Date 2031-05-31**.
- **Delivery Date:** 2026-04-01 (start of 61-day Fixturing Period).
- **Fixturing Period:** April 1, 2026 – May 31, 2026 inclusive — **full abatement** of Basic Rent, Additional Rent, Operating Costs, Management Fee, and Realty Taxes `[SCHEDULE_G_OVERRIDE §1]`.
- **Basic Rent:** $18.50/sf Year 1, escalating at fixed **3.0% compounded annually** to $20.82/sf Year 5 `[SCHEDULE_G_OVERRIDE §3]`. Total Basic Rent over Term = **$9,822,500 CAD**.
- **Tenant Inducement Allowance:** **$500,000 lump sum** ($5.00/sf), claim window 12 months from Commencement (Schedule C §3).
- **Rent Deposit:** $327,666.67 (first month $154,166.67 + final-month security $173,500.00, no interest).
- **Renewal Options:** None (Tenant has no renewal right).
- **No Indemnifier; no Letter of Credit required.**
- **Total Financial Exposure (Basic Rent only):** $9.82M CAD over Term — Additional Rent (Operating Costs, Realty Taxes, Mgmt Fee 5%, Utilities, HST) is incremental and uncapped.
- **Critical Risks:** Landlord retains a **6-month unilateral termination right** (sale/demolition/substantial alteration) at sole, absolute discretion (§17.9); Tenant cannot contest Realty Taxes (§6.6(c)); Operating Costs uncapped with broad capital-amortization inclusion; broad environmental indemnity surviving termination.

The lease departs from a typical landlord-form template materially in **favour of Tenant** in Article 14 (Assignment/Subletting): consent reasonableness limited, recapture deleted, $3,500 consent fee cap, 15-Business-Day deemed consent, 50/50 Net Transfer Premium split AFTER Tenant deductions, full Tenant release on qualifying assignment to a $10M+ TNW assignee.
