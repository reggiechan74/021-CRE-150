This is the economic cost of the incentive package — 3 months free rent plus $15/sf TI consumed $24.29/sf in present value, reducing the effective annuity by one-third of the face rate.

**Offer's "$18.21/sf" Figure**: The offer's own financial summary computes this by simply deducting the 3-month free rent from total base rent and dividing by 5 years × area. It ignores TI cost and leasing commissions entirely. The PRR-adjusted figure ($12.86/sf) is the correct landlord investment metric.

**Incentives as % of Year 1 Gross Rent: 79.21%**: The total incentive package (TI + free rent NPV) equals 79% of one full year of gross income. This is high for a 5-year deal — it's equivalent to giving the tenant nearly a full year of free occupancy.

**Incentive Payback: 9.51 months**: At Year 1 gross income rates, the landlord recovers all incentive costs in under 10 months — a healthy payback for a 5-year deal.

### D. Methodology

**NER formula**: NER = PMT(r, n, NPV(lease deal)/sf) × 12

Where NPV(lease deal)/sf = NPV(rent received)/sf − NPV(all landlord costs)/sf

**PRR Fully Levered Breakeven** = (Dividends on equity) + (Interest on loan) + (Principal payment)
= [dividend_yield × (1−LTV) + interest × LTV + principal_rate × LTV] × acquisition_cost_psf

**Sinking Fund (Inwood method)**: FV annuity payment to accumulate to (40% × acquisition cost)/GLA sf over 18 remaining years at discount rate.

All calculations use monthly compounding with annuity-due (beginning-of-period) convention to match Excel BAF model.
