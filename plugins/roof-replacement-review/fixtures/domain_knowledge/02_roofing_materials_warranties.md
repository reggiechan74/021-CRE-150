# Roofing Materials, Warranty Tiers & Installation Best Practices

Reference document for the `roof-replacement-review` plugin evaluating roofing contractor tender submissions in Ontario, Canada. All material claims should be traceable to the Sources section. Prefer manufacturer technical data sheets and CRCA/NRCA guidance over secondary sources.

> **Scope note:** This document synthesizes manufacturer published warranty and product literature with CRCA/NRCA technical guidance. Warranty terms, contractor program tiers, and product thicknesses change; the plugin should ultimately cite the dated manufacturer PDF (TDS or warranty sample) for any bid it evaluates, not this document alone.

---

## 1. Commercial Low-Slope Membrane Systems

Five system families dominate the Ontario commercial low-slope market: 2-ply SBS modified bitumen, 4-ply built-up roofing (BUR), single-ply TPO, single-ply EPDM, and single-ply PVC (with KEE as a niche thermoplastic subset). Below is what a bid evaluator should expect to see specified for each.

### 1.1 2-Ply SBS Modified Bitumen

- **Typical service life:** Manufacturer claims 25-30 years for premium 2-ply systems (Soprema Sopralene, IKO Armourbond/Torchflex). CRCA and Canadian real-world experience generally observes 20-30 years when properly maintained, with some high-quality installations reaching 30-40 years. Expect a plan-basis number around 25 years for Class A bids.
- **Configuration:** Base sheet + cap sheet (2 plies). Cap sheet is typically granulated; base is smooth. Total nominal membrane thickness should be ~8 mm combined (base ~3 mm, cap ~4-4.5 mm).
- **Attachment methods:** Torch-applied, heat-welded, self-adhered (SA), hot-asphalt mopped, or cold-applied adhesive. Self-adhered and cold-applied are increasingly preferred for occupied buildings to avoid hot work hazards; Soprema's own literature states SA systems achieve equivalent long-term performance to torched.
- **Insulation stack:** Polyiso (typical ASTM C1289 Type II Class 1, ~R-5.7/inch aged LTTR) over steel deck, with a cover board — either high-density (HD) polyiso (ASTM C1289 Type II Class 4) or gypsum-fiber (DensDeck Prime). Thickness typically 3-5 inches of polyiso for Zone 6 Ontario retrofits to approach R-30 to R-35 continuous.
- **Warranty tiers:**
  - **Material-only:** Soprema provides a standard material warranty to any purchaser (10-15 yr typical).
  - **Total System / NDL:** Soprema's **Mammouth Platinum** (cold-applied systems) and **Sopra-Garantie** programs provide system warranties — membrane, insulation, flashing — up to 20-30 years NDL, but only via **PAQ+S** (quality assurance) program contractors.
  - IKO Commercial (Modiflex, Torchflex): system warranties 10/15/20 years via IKO Shield Agent network.
- **Warranty prerequisites:** Certified installer (Soprema PAQ+S, IKO Shield Agent), pre-installation drawing review, mid-job and final inspection by manufacturer technical rep, and payment in full to contractor and manufacturer before guarantee issues.

### 1.2 Built-Up Roofing (4-ply BUR)

- **Typical service life:** 20-30 years typical; CRCA field observations support 30+ with good maintenance. Some sources report 30-40 years when properly installed and maintained.
- **Configuration:** 4 plies of Type IV or Type VI fiberglass felt mopped in hot asphalt, with a flood-coat-and-gravel surfacing or cap sheet. Legacy system — declining in new-construction specification in Ontario but still common for re-roof.
- **Attachment:** Hot asphalt (Type III or IV). Cold-applied BUR exists but is uncommon in Ontario.
- **Insulation stack:** Same as SBS — polyiso + cover board. A cover board is **mandatory** under BUR to protect polyiso facer from hot asphalt damage.
- **Warranty tiers:** Typically 10-20 year material warranties. True NDL coverage is harder to obtain on BUR today as most manufacturers have shifted marketing emphasis to modified bitumen and single-ply.
- **Red flag:** New-build BUR specification in 2026 Ontario without a specific technical rationale (e.g., heritage match) is unusual and should prompt questions.

### 1.3 Single-Ply TPO

- **Typical service life:** Manufacturers claim 20-30 years. Real-world Canadian experience: 15-25 years depending on thickness, UV exposure, and seam quality. Early-generation TPO (pre-2010) had known weathering failures; current formulations perform better.
- **Membrane thickness:**
  - **45 mil:** Residential or light commercial. **Not acceptable for Class A commercial retrofit.**
  - **60 mil:** Minimum commercial standard. Entry tier.
  - **80 mil:** Premium tier. Required by GAF, Carlisle, and others to unlock 25-30 year warranties. **Best practice for flagship commercial.**
  - **90 mil (TPO Plus / Extra):** Carlisle Sure-Weld Extra and similar.
- **Attachment methods:**
  - Mechanically fastened (MF) — plates and screws through membrane into deck. Fast, cost-effective. Billows in high wind if not designed for uplift.
  - Fully adhered (FA) — bonding adhesive over cover board. Cleaner appearance, better uplift performance, required for some FM Global zones.
  - **Induction-welded (RhinoBond, Olybond, ISOweld):** Plates pre-installed in insulation fastening pattern, membrane heat-welded to plates from top. Combines speed of MF with uplift performance of FA.
  - Ballasted — uncommon for TPO; more common for EPDM.
- **Insulation stack:** Polyiso (R-30+ for Zone 6) + HD cover board. Cover board is **mandatory** under TPO to protect membrane from foot traffic punctures and to provide smooth substrate.
- **Warranty tiers (Carlisle Sure-Weld example):**
  - 15-year Golden Seal — 60 mil, standard applicator.
  - 20-year Golden Seal — 60 or 80 mil.
  - 25-year Golden Seal — 80 mil, Authorized Applicator.
  - 30-year Golden Seal — 80 mil, FA or induction-welded, Authorized Applicator with inspection.
- **GAF EverGuard TPO:** Warranties at 20/25/30 years depending on 60/80 mil and installer certification. GAF explicitly links 30-year warranties to 80 mil and certified contractors.
- **Warranty prerequisites:** Factory-trained / Authorized Applicator designation, pre-job notice to manufacturer, mid-job technical inspection (typically for 25-year+), as-built drawings, owner acceptance inspection.

### 1.4 Single-Ply EPDM

- **Typical service life:** 25-30 years manufacturer claim; well-documented real-world performance of 30+ years on ballasted and fully-adhered systems. Oldest of the single-ply chemistries with the longest field data.
- **Membrane thickness:**
  - **45 mil:** Light commercial / entry.
  - **60 mil:** Commercial standard.
  - **90 mil:** Premium (Firestone/Elevate RubberGard Platinum, Carlisle Sure-Seal). Required to unlock 30-year warranty from most manufacturers.
- **Attachment methods:** Fully adhered, mechanically fastened, or ballasted (stone aggregate over loose-laid membrane). Ballast is uncommon in Ontario retrofit due to structural load and wind-scour concerns at building corners.
- **Insulation stack:** Polyiso + cover board (HD polyiso or DensDeck). Ballasted systems can tolerate lower cover board specification since ballast protects membrane.
- **Warranty tiers (Firestone/Elevate Red Shield):**
  - Red Shield Limited — 5 to 20 years depending on thickness and system.
  - Red Shield **Platinum** — 30-year limited, **only available on complete tear-off (no existing roof left in place)**. NDL on materials and labor. Available for FA, MF, or Ballast EPDM at 90 mil.
- **Carlisle Sure-Seal Golden Seal:** Up to 30 years; hail-and-puncture coverage.
- **Installation prerequisites:** Red Shield Licensed contractor (Firestone Master Contractor for top tier); tear-off required for Platinum.

### 1.5 Single-Ply PVC

- **Typical service life:** 20-30 years; strong chemical resistance makes PVC the preferred choice for restaurants (grease exhaust), manufacturing with chemical off-gas, and rooftop planter applications.
- **Membrane thickness:** Sika Sarnafil is sold in **guaranteed thicknesses** — 48, 60, 72, 80, and 96 mil. Sika publishes a thickness guarantee: if ASTM D-751 testing finds the membrane below the labeled thickness, Sika remunerates $0.05/sq ft (to $5,000 max). This is the gold standard against "bait thickness" bids.
- **Attachment methods:** Mechanically fastened, fully adhered, or induction-welded (Sarnafil SarnaBar, Sikaplan RhinoBond-compatible).
- **Insulation stack:** Polyiso + cover board. PVC is sensitive to certain plasticizer-incompatible materials (asphalt, polystyrene) — the cover board isolates it.
- **Warranty tiers:**
  - **Sarnafil (premium line):** Up to 30-year NDL.
  - **Sikaplan (value line):** Up to 20-year system warranty.
- **Prerequisite:** Sika Sarnafil Authorized Applicator for any warranty over 15 years.

### 1.6 KEE (Ketone Ethylene Ester)

- KEE is a thermoplastic single-ply positioned between TPO and PVC. Seaman FiberTite is the best-known brand; offers excellent chemical resistance. Membrane thicknesses 36/45/50 mil (FiberTite's weave reinforcement means its mil-for-mil performance exceeds homogeneous TPO).
- Warranties up to 30 years. Niche product in Ontario; less common than TPO/PVC/EPDM.

## 2. Residential Steep-Slope Systems

### 2.1 Asphalt Shingles

Three tiers, distinguished by layer construction and aesthetics:

- **3-tab** — single-layer, ~60-70 lb/square. Lowest cost, 25-year limited warranty typical. Largely phased out of new residential; mainly for investor/low-cost repaints. Wind warranty typically 60-70 mph.
- **Architectural / Laminate / Dimensional** — 2 or more laminated layers, ~240-280 lb/square. Dominant residential tier. Examples:
  - GAF **Timberline HDZ** — lifetime limited, 15-year WindProven (no max wind speed with LayerLock technology + StrikeZone + qualifying accessories).
  - CertainTeed **Landmark** — lifetime limited.
  - IKO **Cambridge / Dynasty** — lifetime limited.
  - Malarkey **Vista / Legacy** — lifetime limited, with polymer-modified SBS asphalt (impact and cold-weather advantage in Ontario climate).
  - Owens Corning **TruDefinition Duration** — lifetime limited, SureNail strip.
- **Premium / Designer** — thicker, multi-layer, often simulating slate or shake. Examples: GAF **Grand Sequoia / Camelot II**, CertainTeed **Grand Manor / Presidential**, Malarkey **Windsor XL**. Lifetime limited warranty.

**Warranty reality check:** "Lifetime limited" means lifetime-limited to a non-prorated window (typically 10 years from install) then prorates heavily. Wind warranties require specific accessory usage (starter strip, hip/ridge, underlayment, attic ventilation). Most manufacturers require full system install to honor wind coverage — a red flag when a bid uses mismatched starter/cap from a different brand.

**Transfer:** GAF and Owens Corning allow one full transfer within the non-prorated window. CertainTeed transfer entitles second owner to 40 or 50 years of coverage. IKO charges $100 transfer fee. Architect/consultant should verify transferability is documented in bid if owner is selling.

### 2.2 Metal Roofing

- **Standing seam (concealed fastener):** Premium residential and light commercial. 24 gauge steel is the structural floor for quality standing seam; 22 gauge available for commercial. Aluminum (0.032"-0.040") for coastal/chloride. Copper (16-20 oz) for heritage.
- **Exposed fastener / through-fastened (R-panel, 5V-crimp):** Budget metal. Fastener gasket failure is the primary wear mode; expect to re-gasket at 15-20 years. 26 or 29 gauge typical.
- **Paint systems:**
  - **PVDF (Kynar 500 / Hylar 5000):** 30-40 year paint warranty typical; best fade/chalk resistance; generally only offered on 24-gauge (standing seam). The benchmark for quality metal.
  - **SMP (Silicone-Modified Polyester):** 20-30 year warranty. Chalks and fades faster than PVDF, especially in darker colors. Typically on 26-gauge exposed-fastener product.
  - Polyester: 10-15 year; budget.
- **Substrate warranty** (separate from paint): typically 25-50 years on perforation due to corrosion for Galvalume/AZ50 steel.
- **Canadian relevance:** Snow guards at entrances (code-implicit, commonly omitted in bids) and ice-belt detailing at eaves are critical.

### 2.3 Synthetic Slate & Composite Shake

- **DaVinci Roofscapes** (polymer slate/shake) — limited lifetime warranty, non-prorated 10 years, then prorated 40 years. Transferable twice in first 10 years. Realistic service life 40-50 years.
- **Brava Roof Tile** (polymer slate/shake/tile) — 50-year limited, non-prorated first 10 years, fully transferable.
- **Euroshield** (recycled rubber shake) — 50-year material warranty. Canadian-made (Calgary). Strong Ontario presence.
- **Enviroshake** — Canadian composite shake, 50-year warranty.

Synthetic slate is typically 30-40% of the cost of natural slate with similar longevity and far lower dead load (important on older Ontario homes with uncertain structural capacity for natural slate).

### 2.4 Cedar Shake / Shingle

- Service life 20-30 years for shingles, 30-50 years for hand-split shakes, heavily dependent on exposure and maintenance. Ontario humidity and freeze-thaw shorten life versus western Canada.
- Typically Western Red Cedar (premium) or Eastern White Cedar. Grade 1 (100% edge grain, all heartwood) is the commercial standard; Grade 2 is mixed and compromises longevity.
- Requires pressure-treated (CCA or ACQ) for fire rating — check local bylaws; some Ontario municipalities restrict untreated cedar in built-up areas.
- Manufacturer warranties are typically limited (10-25 years) and heavily dependent on certified installer.

### 2.5 Underlayment

- **Synthetic underlayment** (woven polypropylene/polyethylene) — replaces traditional #15 and #30 asphalt felt as field underlayment. Better tear resistance, UV-stable for weeks, lighter. Examples: GAF Deck-Armor, Owens Corning ProArmor, Titanium UDL, Sharkskin.
- **Asphalt-saturated felt (#15, #30)** — legacy. Still acceptable but loses ground each year.
- **Ice & water shield (self-adhered polymer-modified bitumen):** Code-required in Ontario at eaves, valleys, penetrations. OBC requires extension **at least 24 inches inside the interior plane of the exterior wall** (i.e., past the heated-wall line). Products: GAF WeatherWatch / StormGuard, Grace Ice & Water Shield, IKO StormShield, CertainTeed WinterGuard.
- **Best practice:** Ice & water at all vulnerable zones + synthetic in the field. Using only felt in field on Ontario project is a yellow flag in 2026.

## 3. Manufacturer Certified Contractor Programs

These programs are the mechanism by which manufacturers transfer risk (labor and workmanship coverage) to contractors and unlock top-tier warranties for owners. Non-certified installers cannot sell the top-tier warranties — regardless of installation quality.

### 3.1 GAF (Residential + Commercial)

| Program | Qualifies | Warranty Unlocked |
|---|---|---|
| GAF Certified | Licensed, insured, good reputation | System Plus (non-prorated period extended) |
| GAF Master Elite | 3+ yr Certified history, proven quality, additional factory training. **< 2% of North American roofers.** No new contractors added without seasoning. | **Golden Pledge** — lifetime material + **25-year workmanship** + tear-off/disposal + misapplication coverage. Transferable. |

**Owner receives:** With Master Elite + Golden Pledge, the owner is covered for contractor workmanship errors for 25 years — GAF will pay to fix installation defects, which a standard material-only warranty excludes. This is the single biggest warranty differentiator in residential asphalt.

### 3.2 Firestone / Elevate (Holcim)

| Program | Qualifies | Warranty Unlocked |
|---|---|---|
| Red Shield Licensed | Factory trained | Red Shield Limited 5-20 yr |
| Master Contractor | Volume + quality thresholds | Red Shield 20-25 yr |
| **Platinum Master Contractor** | **Top 1% of Red Shield Licensed** | Red Shield **Platinum** 30-yr NDL, edge-to-edge, transferable |

**Key restriction:** Red Shield Platinum requires **complete tear-off** — no recover over existing membrane.

### 3.3 Soprema (SBS leader in Canada)

### 3.5 Carlisle SynTec

Authorized Applicator program gates any warranty beyond 15 years. Sika's thickness-guarantee program (remuneration if membrane measures thin) is the most owner-protective provision in the single-ply market and a useful comparator when evaluating other brands' TDS claims.

## 4. Key Evaluation Red Flags

When reviewing a tender submission, these patterns warrant immediate follow-up:

1. **Mismatched system components.** "Brand A membrane with Brand B adhesive / Brand C fastener plate" voids most system warranties. Manufacturers require approved accessories (adhesive, primer, seam tape, termination bar, sealant) to be from the same brand family. A bid that lists membrane cost without explicitly naming the adhesive or fastener is hiding this risk.
2. **Sub-specification membrane thickness for the use case.** 45 mil TPO or EPDM on a Class A commercial retrofit is a red flag — 60 mil is the floor, 80 mil is the standard for 25-30 year warranties.
3. **Missing cover board.** Polyiso alone under TPO/PVC/EPDM is best-practice-deficient. ASTM C1289 Type II Class 4 HD polyiso or DensDeck Prime gypsum-fiber cover board over the polyiso is the current standard. Most manufacturer 25-year+ warranties require it.
4. **No wind uplift design basis.** Bids must state the design basis — ASCE 7 (current edition IBC) pressures for the specific building, or FM 1-60 / 1-75 / 1-90 if FM-insured. A generic "meets code" without a calculated uplift pressure for field/perimeter/corner zones is incomplete. NRCA publishes a free Roof Wind Designer tool (roofwinddesigner.nrca.net) usable for verification.
5. **"25-year warranty" without the type.** Material-only, labor-and-material, or NDL Total System — these are radically different products priced similarly by weaker bidders. The bid must specify. Prorated vs non-prorated period must be stated.
6. **Missing workmanship warranty.** A manufacturer material warranty does NOT cover installation defects (leaks from improper flashing, loose fasteners, bad seams). The contractor's own workmanship warranty is separate. Typical range: 2-10 years; 5 years is market median in Ontario for reputable contractors. A bid without a stated workmanship term is incomplete.
7. **No pre-installation or mid-job manufacturer inspection for long warranties.** 25-30 year warranties almost universally require manufacturer technical rep site visits. If the bid doesn't include manufacturer inspection fees, the top-tier warranty won't issue — even if the contractor is certified.
8. **Recover (overlay) with Red Shield Platinum or equivalent NDL specified.** Platinum-tier 30-year warranties typically require full tear-off. Bidder cannot deliver both a recover scope AND a Platinum warranty.
9. **Stated R-value below current OBC SB-12.** Ontario retrofits under OBC 2024/SB-12 should target R-30 to R-35+ continuous on commercial low-slope roofs depending on climate zone and compliance path. Bid showing R-20 on a mid-Ontario retrofit implies the contractor didn't price to code.
10. **Paint warranty listed without separating substrate and paint.** Metal roofs have two warranties: substrate (perforation/corrosion, typically 25-50 yr Galvalume) and paint finish (PVDF 30-40 yr, SMP 20-30 yr). Consolidating them into one number hides which component fails first.

## 5. Common Scope Omissions

Bids frequently exclude these items. A non-exhaustive list of what to verify:

- **Wood deck replacement allowance.** Bids should include a per-board-foot unit price for rotten deck replacement discovered on tear-off (e.g., "$X/bf up to Y bf included; additional billed at unit price"). Without this, change orders are inevitable.
- **Insulation R-value upgrade to code (SB-12 compliance).** On retrofits, OBC may require bringing insulation up to current code rather than matching existing. Bid should state the target R-value and the SB-12 compliance path used.
- **Metal flashing gauge and finish.** "Metal flashing included" is inadequate. Specify gauge (24 ga min for commercial counterflashing) and finish (Kynar 500, color-matched). Parapets, counterflashings, reglets, and drip edges are separate line items in disciplined bids.
- **Roof drain reconditioning vs replacement.** Existing drains frequently need new clamping rings, strainers, or complete drain body replacement. Cast iron drain bodies often hide cracks until disturbed.
- **Lightning protection (LPS) reconnection.** If building has an LPS (CSA B72), tear-off disconnects it. Bid must include licensed LPS contractor to re-bond after the new roof.
- **HVAC curb raising and flashing.** When insulation thickness increases under new code, HVAC curbs often need extension/raising. Curb flashings are a high-failure area.
- **Skylight replacement or reglazing.** Skylights older than the roof are usually past service life. Re-roofing around old skylights is false economy; bid should offer replacement option.
- **Snow guards over entrances (metal roofs).** Code-implicit liability item — bid should include at minimum over egress doors.
- **Gas line, antenna, satellite, solar mount re-flashing.** Each rooftop penetration needs new counterflashing. Easy to miss in a visual estimate.
- **Walk pads / protection mats** at service paths, HVAC units, and roof access points — prolongs membrane life and is an NRCA best practice.
- **Tie-in to adjacent roof sections** of different age/system. Transition detailing is a leak-prone area.
- **Disposal / tipping fees and permits** — sometimes itemized, sometimes buried in mobilization.
- **Temporary protection / night seals during multi-day work.** Should be standard but is occasionally "as-required" with no allowance.
- **Interior protection** below work area (dust, falling debris) — particularly relevant for occupied commercial.

## 6. Quick Evaluation Matrix (Plugin Reference)

| Criterion | Pass | Flag | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| TPO thickness (commercial) | 80 mil | 60 mil | <60 mil |
| EPDM thickness (commercial) | 90 mil | 60 mil | 45 mil |
| Cover board | HD polyiso or DensDeck | Standard polyiso only | No cover board |
| Wind design basis | ASCE 7 calc or FM rating stated | "Meets code" generic | Not mentioned |
| Warranty type | NDL Total System | Material + labor | Material-only |
| Contractor certification | Manufacturer certified at required tier | Licensed but uncertified | No cert, seeking 25yr+ warranty |
| Workmanship warranty | 5-10 yr stated | 2 yr | Not stated |
| Deck replacement | Unit-price allowance | Lump-sum allowance | Excluded |
| Insulation R-value | Meets/exceeds OBC SB-12 | Matches existing | Below current code |
