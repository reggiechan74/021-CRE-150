## CAM Pool Structure

Per the standard lease form and the Management Agreement, recoverable operating expenses are allocated to two pools:

| Pool | Rentable Area | Pool Share |
|------|--------------:|-----------:|
| Office Pool | 42,000 sf | 67.74% |
| Retail Pool | 20,000 sf | 32.26% |
| **Total** | **62,000 sf** | **100.00%** |

**Pool-specific items** (not shared):

- **Office pool only:** Elevator maintenance, office HVAC service, office-floor janitorial scope
- **Retail pool only:** Storefront glass cleaning, sidewalk/façade maintenance, restaurant grease-trap servicing, snow removal for retail loading zone

**Tenant-level variations:**

- **CanadaFirst Bank (Unit 101):** CAM charges subject to 4% annual cap over base-year CAM ($11.40/sf in 2022). FY2025 cap ceiling: $12.82/sf.
- **Matheson Family Dental (Unit 103):** Base year 2024 for realty tax and operating expenses. Pays only increases above 2024 baseline.
- **Peak Fitness Studio (Unit 104):** Modified gross. **Excluded** from utilities and R&M allocations. Pays pro-rata share of realty tax, insurance, landscaping, security, snow removal, management fee only.
- **Coverpoint Insurance (Suite 310):** Base year 2023.
- **Pronto Italian (Unit 105):** Net lease but lease Schedule C specifically excludes grease-trap, ventilation, and hood-cleaning costs from recoveries (tenant self-performs and pays directly).

## Key Lease-Level Exclusions from Recoverable Operating Expenses

Per the form lease, Article 6.03 excludes the following from the CAM pool:

1. Capital expenditures (except amortized over useful life per Article 6.04)
2. Leasing commissions, tenant inducements, tenant improvement allowances
3. **One-time extraordinary charges related to tenant turnover** (move-out cleaning, suite restoration, demising wall work)
4. Legal fees related to leasing, financing, or ownership disputes
5. Income taxes, capital taxes, transfer taxes
6. Expenses recovered from insurance, warranties, or third parties
7. Costs of correcting defects in original construction or latent defects

## Management Fee Clause (Excerpt — Management Agreement §4.1)

> "As compensation for services rendered hereunder, Owner shall pay Manager a monthly management fee equal to four percent (4.0%) of the **Effective Gross Income** of the Property for such month. For the purposes of this Section, '**Effective Gross Income**' means the sum of all rental income, additional rent, parking revenue, and other miscellaneous income actually billed in respect of the Property, *less* (i) vacancy loss attributable to unleased space, and (ii) an allowance for credit losses and uncollected rent, in each case determined in accordance with generally accepted property management practice."

*This definition is the focal point of Exercise 3. The Property Manager's FY2025 posting applied 4% to Gross Potential Income rather than Effective Gross Income. The discrepancy is recoverable by the landlord via a PM fee credit but is **not** chargeable to tenants as part of CAM.*

## Annual Cycle — CAM Reconciliation Timing

| Event | Timing |
|-------|--------|
| Prior-year CAM budget finalized (basis for tenant monthly pre-billings) | Dec of prior year |
| Monthly CAM pre-bills issued to tenants | 1st of each month |
| Year-end close (GL) | 2nd week of January following year |
| Draft CAM reconciliation | February–March following year |
| **Final reconciliation statements issued to tenants** | **By March 31 following year (lease requirement)** |
| Tenant dispute window | 90 days from statement receipt |
| True-up billing or credit | Q2 of following year |
