## BINDING NATURE OF OFFER

**NON-BINDING BUSINESS TERMS**: This Offer to Lease sets forth preliminary business terms and is **NOT a binding lease agreement**. The business terms outlined below — Premises, Term, Rent, Additional Rent, Tenant Improvements, Free Rent, Security Deposit, and Renewal Option — do **NOT** create legally enforceable obligations.

**SUBJECT TO FORMAL LEASE**: All business terms are subject to negotiation and execution of a formal Lease Agreement using Landlord's standard form industrial lease as the basis.

**BINDING PROVISIONS**: The following provisions **ARE binding**:
- Exclusivity
- Good Faith Negotiation
- Deposit
- Confidentiality
- Acceptance and Irrevocability
- Conditions Precedent (procedural terms only)
- Lease Execution Deadline

---

## EXCLUSIVITY (BINDING)

**Exclusivity Period**: For **45 days** from Landlord's acceptance, Landlord agrees to:
1. Remove the Premises from the market
2. Not negotiate lease terms with any other party for the Premises
3. Negotiate the formal Lease exclusively with Tenant in good faith

**Landlord's Breach**: If Landlord leases the Premises to another party during the Exclusivity Period, Landlord shall immediately refund the Deposit and reimburse Tenant's reasonable due diligence costs (maximum $15,000).

**Expiry**: The earlier of 45 days from acceptance, execution of the formal Lease, or automatic termination under Conditions Precedent.

---

## GOOD FAITH NEGOTIATION (BINDING)

Parties agree to negotiate the formal Lease in good faith based on the terms of this Offer.

**Good Faith Standard**:
1. Respond to drafts and comments within **5 business days**
2. Use Landlord's standard form industrial lease as starting point
3. Not seek material changes to business terms (rent, term, TI, free rent) set out in this Offer

**Breach Consequences**:
- If Tenant breaches: Deposit forfeited to Landlord
- If Landlord breaches: Deposit refunded plus reimbursement of due diligence costs (maximum $15,000)

## RENEWAL OPTION (NON-BINDING)

**Number of Options**: One (1) renewal option

**Renewal Term**: 5 years (March 1, 2031 – February 28, 2036)

**Renewal Rent**: Fair Market Rent as of the renewal commencement date, for comparable industrial premises in the Mississauga Southdown submarket

**Fair Market Rent Process**:
1. Landlord provides written FMR proposal **12 months** prior to expiry
2. Parties negotiate FMR for **60 days**
3. If no agreement, binding arbitration by two licensed industrial appraisers (one per party); if no agreement, third appraiser appointed jointly; FMR based on comparable premises within 5 km radius of similar size and quality
4. Arbitration costs: each party pays its own appraiser; third appraiser split 50/50

**Notice to Exercise**: Written notice required **12 months** prior to Lease expiry (time of the essence)

**Conditions**: No monetary defaults at time of notice or renewal commencement; no more than one material non-monetary default during the preceding term.

**No Tenant Incentives**: Renewal is for Base Rent and Additional Rent only. No free rent, TI, or other inducements unless mutually agreed in writing.
