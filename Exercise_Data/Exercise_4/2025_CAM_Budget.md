## Budget Assumptions & Notes

### Realty Tax ($420,000)
- Based on 2024 final tax bill of $398,200, uplifted 5.5% for expected MPAC phase-in and municipal rate increase.
- **MPAC reassessment cycle:** Ontario paused the 2020 reassessment cycle; next reassessment expected for the 2026 tax year. No reassessment assumed in FY2025 budget.
- *Note: reassessment risk flagged in budget notes as "low probability for 2025" — see variance discussion in actual GL.*

### Utilities ($195,000)
- Electric (Alectra): $115,000 — based on FY2024 actual $108,500 + 6% rate inflation
- Gas (Enbridge): $62,000 — based on FY2024 actual $58,400 + 6% + colder-winter contingency
- Water (Region of Peel): $18,000

### R&M ($115,000)
- Elevator service contract (Kone): $22,000 — office pool only
- HVAC preventive maintenance: $28,000
- General building R&M (plumbing, electrical, carpentry): $38,000
- Roof inspections & minor repairs: $7,000
- Pest control: $4,000
- Restaurant-specific (grease trap, kitchen exhaust): $4,500 — retail pool only, allocated away from Pronto per lease
- Retail storefront glass cleaning: $6,000 — retail pool only
- Contingency: $5,500

### Management Fee ($108,000)
- 4.0% × Projected Effective Gross Income of $2,700,000

- **Fee basis defined per Management Agreement §4.1 as Effective Gross Income, not Gross Potential Income.**

- **Exclusion:** Lease Article 6.03(c) explicitly excludes one-time extraordinary charges related to tenant turnover from this category. Budget assumes no tenant moves requiring extraordinary cleaning.

## Purpose of This Document

This budget is the **basis against which actual FY2025 operating expenses are reconciled**. It was also used to set the monthly CAM pre-bills above.

At year-end reconciliation (now, April 2026), the process is:

1. Compare actual FY2025 recoverable opex (from GL) against this budget → identify variances
2. For each variance, determine: legitimate variance? classification error? accounting mistake?
3. Apply lease-specific exclusions, caps, base-year adjustments, and tenant-specific allocations
4. True-up: if actual > pre-billed, bill tenants the difference; if actual < pre-billed, credit tenants
5. Issue final reconciliation statements to tenants by March 31 (LATE — we are past this date)

**The Property Manager's draft reconciliation (from the FY2025 GL extract) shows opex running $96,692 over budget ($1,178,692 actual vs $1,082,000 budget). Audit that number.**
