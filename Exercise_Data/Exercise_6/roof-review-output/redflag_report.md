- Recommended action: clarify

**[CRITICAL] Substitutions — VE Option 5 proposes omitting cover board on a mechanically attached TPO over polyiso assembly. Cover board omission on commercial polyiso is a known critical deficiency (protects membrane from polyiso facer degradation, fastener backout, and hail). - Citation: fixture 02 section 4 (cover board on polyiso); CRCA bulletin on cover boards
