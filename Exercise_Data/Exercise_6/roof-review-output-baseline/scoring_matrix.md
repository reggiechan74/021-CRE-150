## Scoring Rationale

### 1. Heritage Construction Group — 79.88 pts

**Strengths:** Top-tier manufacturer certifications (GAF Master Elite, Carlisle Master Installer, Firestone Premier) with 14 yrs in business; Detailed three-phase sequencing for occupied pharma-warehouse with 72-hr HVAC coordination and daily watertight commitments; Four commercial references within 5 years, including Apotex pharma as a near-identical scope analogue
**Weaknesses:** Warranty is membrane-only NDL, not the total_system_ndl the RFP requires; no named manufacturer system-warranty program for the base bid; Wind-uplift design basis absent, fall-protection plan only program-level, crew size and mobilization-days-after-award not quantified
**Key differentiator:** Stacked top-tier manufacturer certifications (including GAF Master Elite) paired with a disciplined three-phase occupied-site plan — but warranty scope and base-bid manufacturer commitment are the catch.

### 2. Summit Contracting Ltd. — 76.95 pts

**Strengths:** Best-in-class materials/warranty stack: 80-mil TPO + HD cover board + 30-yr total-system NDL from GAF Master Elite / Carlisle Master Installer / Firestone Premier; Comprehensive occupied-building protocol for pharma-warehouse: 3-phase sequencing (max 8,000 sf exposed), wireless ±2°C temperature monitoring, and 72-hr HVAC coordination; Pfizer Canada (2025, 95k sf Mississauga pharma) and three other recent commercial references provide direct-analog evidence for this scope
**Weaknesses:** Mandatory-gate documentation gaps (addenda ack, non-collusion declaration, performance/L&M bond references, CGL additional-insured / completed-ops language, WSIB date/good-standing text) — five gates currently needs_clarification; Schedule and crew details incomplete: mobilization lead-time and total crew headcount not stated; fall-protection plan lacks site-specific anchor-point engineering
**Key differentiator:** Summit couples a top-tier 30-year total-system NDL warranty on 80-mil TPO with the most complete occupied-pharma phasing and temperature-monitoring protocol of any bidder, backed by a direct Pfizer Mississauga reference.

### 3. Pinnacle Roofing Corp. — 76.29 pts

**Strengths:** Three comparable recent commercial references (Canadian Tire 2024, Loblaws 2023, Tim Hortons 2025) directly in-scope and in-class.; 60 mil fully-adhered TPO with gypsum cover board and two-layer R-30 polyiso fully meets the RFP system spec with no substitutions.; Three-phase occupied-site plan with max 10,000 sqft exposed (tighter than 15k RFP cap) and twice-weekly tenant coordination.
**Weaknesses:** 20-year NDL warranty is not a total-system NDL — membrane-scope language only, short of what the RFP requires (critical red flag).; Membrane manufacturer not committed (three certifications listed, none selected), wind-uplift design basis missing, and fall-protection plan is generic on a 47-penetration occupied roof.
**Key differentiator:** Deep commercial reference stack and RFP-spec-matching materials, but the warranty tier gap and un-named manufacturer undercut an otherwise compliant technical package.
