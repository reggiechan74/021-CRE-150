## 1. Recommendation

We recommend award of the roof replacement contract to **Heritage Construction Group** for a base bid of **$578,000 CAD** (excluding HST); with 13% HST, the all-in contract value is **$653,140**.

Heritage Construction Group achieved the highest weighted score of 79.88/100 against the RFP §7 evaluation criteria, leading the second-ranked compliant bid by 2.93 points. Of the five submissions received, three are compliant or compliant-with-clarifications and two are non-compliant on material grounds (see §4).

Award is conditional on the owner resolving the clarifications enumerated in §5 before contract execution.

---

## 2. Why Not the Low Bid

The two lowest bids, Metro Building Solutions at $468,000 and Lakeside Roofing at $487,500, are both disqualified as non-compliant. Their apparent savings vs Heritage Construction Group ($110,000 and $90,500 respectively) are engineered by deleting RFP scope items that would void the manufacturer system warranty, fail RFP §3.1 material specifications, or fail RFP §4.3 phasing requirements for an occupied pharmaceutical warehouse. Awarding to either would expose the owner to uninsured warranty failure within the 20-year design life.

Among compliant bids, Heritage Construction Group is in fact the low compliant bid at $578,000; Pinnacle Roofing at $612,000 is +5.9% and Summit Contracting at $742,000 is +28.4%. The recommendation therefore does not carry a compliant-bid price premium.

## 5. Award Conditions

Prior to contract execution, Heritage Construction Group must provide or satisfy the following:

1. Certificate of Insurance endorsed to name Tenebrus Capital as additional insured, with 2-year completed-operations tail in writing from insurer.
2. Signed acknowledgment of all RFP addenda on Form of Tender.
3. Signed and dated non-collusion declaration.
4. **Commit the membrane manufacturer and product line** for the 60 mil TPO base bid in writing (GAF EverGuard, Carlisle Sure-Weld, Firestone UltraPly, or equivalent from the contractor's certified list). Warranty tier (total-system NDL) and wind-uplift calculation are contingent on this commitment.
5. **Provide the total-system NDL warranty document** naming Tenebrus Capital and transferable to a future owner per RFP §9.6, or acknowledge in writing that the offered warranty is membrane-NDL only (in which case this recommendation must be re-evaluated).
6. **Provide the wind-uplift design basis** (CSA A123.21 test assembly or FM 1-29 rating) demonstrating the fully-adhered TPO-over-polyiso-over-steel-deck assembly meets the uplift demand for the site (4B terrain, Mississauga, 28 ft mean roof height).
7. **Submit a site-specific fall-protection plan** per CSA Z91 naming anchor points, parapet strategy, tie-off equipment, and the certified Working-at-Heights (O. Reg. 297/13) roster for the assigned crew.
8. **Clarify VE Option 5** (cover board deletion) with written manufacturer confirmation that the proposed system retains its NDL warranty without a cover board over polyiso, or withdraw this option from the bid. Absent confirmation, the owner should reject this VE option; deleting cover board on commercial polyiso voids most manufacturer system warranties per CRCA guidance.
9. **Confirm the manufacturer certification program** tier held (GAF Master Elite, Carlisle Master Installer, Firestone Red Shield, or equivalent) matches the certified installer required for the total-system NDL warranty at the selected manufacturer.

**Standard conditions (applied to every award):**
- 10% statutory holdback under Ontario *Construction Act*; monthly progress certification.
- Substantial performance declaration at 97% per *Construction Act* s. 2, with publication per s. 32.
- Manufacturer inspection within 30 days of substantial performance; inspector's sign-off is a condition of final warranty issuance.
- Change orders priced at actual cost +10% self-performed / +5% subcontracted (RFP §9.4).
- Liquidated damages of $500/calendar day for failure to achieve substantial completion by July 31, 2026 (RFP §9.5).

## 7. Contract Form
