### PHASING PLAN SUMMARY

Our phasing approach is designed specifically for occupied, temperature-sensitive facilities:

**Phase 1 (Days 1-25):** Office wing roof (5,100 sq ft)
- Complete isolation from main roof
- Minimal disruption to office operations
- Establishes workflow patterns for main roof

**Phase 2 (Days 26-55):** Main roof - Northwest quadrant (20,000 sq ft)
- Coordinate with warehouse operations schedule
- Temporary weather protection each evening
- Temperature monitoring throughout

**Phase 3 (Days 56-85):** Main roof - Southeast quadrant including ponding areas (25,000 sq ft)
- Tapered insulation system installed
- Additional drain if required (included in allowance)
- Final tie-ins to office wing

**Key Phasing Protections:**
- Maximum 8,000 sq ft exposed at any time (well under 15,000 sq ft RFP limit)
- Each phase made watertight daily by 5:00 PM
- Weekend protection: reinforced tarps with weighted edges
- Temperature monitoring: wireless sensors in warehouse, alerts if temperature deviates ±2°C
- HVAC coordination: 72-hour notice minimum, shutdowns scheduled for weekends only

### VALUE ENGINEERING OBSERVATIONS

Based on our site visit and review of the RFP, we offer the following observations:

1. **R-38 vs. R-30:** We have priced R-38 as standard. The incremental cost over R-30 is approximately $24,000, but energy savings typically provide 5-7 year payback. Given the warehouse is temperature-sensitive, the additional insulation provides operational cost benefits.

2. **80 mil vs. 60 mil membrane:** For a roof of this complexity (multiple levels, many penetrations), the additional puncture resistance of 80 mil membrane provides better long-term value.

3. **Ponding correction:** We have included tapered insulation for the identified ponding areas. This is critical - ponding voids most manufacturer warranties.

4. **Fully adhered vs. mechanically attached:** For this building height and exposure, fully adhered provides superior wind uplift resistance. Given the pharmaceutical tenant and temperature sensitivity, we recommend the additional security.
