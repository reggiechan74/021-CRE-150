## VALUE ENGINEERING PROPOSALS

We have identified the following value engineering opportunities for your consideration:

### VE OPTION 1: Mechanically Attached System
**Save $42,000**

Instead of fully adhered membrane, use mechanically attached system.

- **Cost Impact:** -$42,000 (reduced adhesive costs and faster installation)
- **Performance Impact:** Minimal for this building height (28 ft) and sheltered industrial location
- **Warranty Impact:** Same 20-year NDL warranty available
- **Trade-off:** Slightly more vulnerable to extreme wind events; marginally shorter expected service life (25-28 years vs. 30+ years)
- **Our Analysis:** For a single-story industrial building in Mississauga with parapet walls, wind uplift risk is moderate. Mechanically attached systems are widely used in similar applications.

**Our Recommendation:** ACCEPTABLE for this building. The risk profile is low given building height and location. Savings can be redirected to other upgrades.

---

### VE Option 2: Reduced Insulation Configuration
**Save $18,000**

Use single-layer R-30 insulation instead of two-layer R-30 with staggered joints.

- **Cost Impact:** -$18,000 (reduced material and labour)
- **Performance Impact:** Same R-value achieved, but single layer has more thermal bridging at joints
- **Energy Impact:** Estimated 5-8% increase in heating/cooling costs vs. two-layer staggered system
- **Annual Energy Penalty:** Approximately $1,200-$1,800/year for this building
- **Code Compliance:** Single layer meets Ontario Building Code minimum

**Our Recommendation:** NOT RECOMMENDED. The two-layer staggered joint system provides meaningfully better thermal performance. Energy penalty exceeds savings within 10-15 years. Given the tenant is temperature-sensitive, the additional thermal consistency is valuable.

---

### VE Option 3: Premium Membrane Upgrade
**Add $38,000**

Upgrade from 60 mil to 80 mil TPO membrane.

- **Cost Impact:** +$38,000
- **Performance Impact:** 33% more material thickness, better puncture resistance, improved hail rating
- **Warranty Impact:** Upgrades to 30-year NDL warranty
- **Service Life:** Expected 35+ years vs. 25-30 years for 60 mil
- **Particularly Relevant:** This building has 47 penetrations and three roof levels - more seam length and detailing means more potential vulnerability points

**Our Recommendation:** RECOMMENDED for owners planning long-term hold. For a complex roof like this one (multiple levels, many penetrations, ponding history), the additional membrane thickness provides meaningful durability improvement. Incremental cost of $38,000 over 35+ year life = approximately $1,085/year.

---

### VE Option 4: Enhanced Insulation Package
**Add $24,000**

Upgrade from R-30 to R-38 insulation.

- **Cost Impact:** +$24,000
- **Energy Impact:** Estimated 18-22% reduction in roof-related heating/cooling costs
- **Annual Energy Savings:** Approximately $4,200-$5,800/year (based on current energy rates)
- **Payback Period:** 4-6 years
- **Additional Benefits:** 
  - Better temperature stability for pharmaceutical tenant
  - Reduced HVAC equipment cycling
  - Potential utility rebates (Enbridge and Union Gas offer up to $8,000 for commercial insulation upgrades)
  - Improved comfort in office wing

**Our Recommendation:** STRONGLY RECOMMENDED. Excellent ROI with 4-6 year payback. Particularly valuable given the temperature-sensitive tenant. We can assist with rebate applications.

---

### VE Option 5: Combined Optimal Package
**Net Add $12,000**

Combine VE Option 1 (mechanically attached) + VE Option 3 (80 mil membrane) + VE Option 4 (R-38 insulation).

- **Cost Impact:** -$42,000 + $38,000 + $24,000 = +$20,000
- **Additional Savings:** Remove cover board (not required with mechanically attached assembly per manufacturer) = -$8,000
- **Net Cost Impact:** +$12,000

**Results in:**
- 80 mil membrane (upgrade from 60 mil)
- R-38 insulation (upgrade from R-30)
- Mechanically attached system (acceptable alternative)
- No cover board (acceptable with this assembly per manufacturer)
- 30-year NDL warranty

**Our Recommendation:** BEST VALUE. For only $12,000 additional (2% of total project cost), owner receives:
- Significantly upgraded membrane (critical for complex roof with many penetrations)
- Improved insulation (valuable for temperature-sensitive tenant)
- Extended warranty coverage
- Better long-term lifecycle cost
