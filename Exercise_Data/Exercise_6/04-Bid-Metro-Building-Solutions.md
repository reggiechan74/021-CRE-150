### IMPORTANT EXCLUSIONS - PLEASE READ CAREFULLY

The following items are **NOT included** in our base bid price and will be charged separately as required:

1. **Steel deck repairs** - $185/hour for labour plus materials (estimated $25,000-$45,000 based on visual inspection)

2. **Deck fastening** - If deck requires additional fastening to structure: $8,500 allowance

3. **Wood deck replacement (office wing)** - Not included. Estimated $15,000-$25,000 if required.

4. **Cover board** - Not included in base bid. Optional upgrade at $38,000. **Note:** We typically do not install cover board on industrial buildings of this age unless specifically required by owner.

5. **Tapered insulation for ponding** - Not included. Optional upgrade at $22,000-$35,000 depending on solution. **Note:** Ponding observed during site visit appears to be superficial. We recommend addressing only if leaks occur.

6. **Drain replacement** - Any drain bowls or leaders requiring replacement: $2,400 per drain (estimated 6-8 drains may need replacement)

7. **HVAC curb modifications** - Any modifications or replacements: $3,200 per unit (estimated 4-6 curbs may require work)

8. **Skylight work** - Skylight replacement or repairs: $4,800 per skylight (12 skylights on roof per RFP - we counted 4 during site visit)

9. **Fascia repairs** - Any fascia replacement required: $145/linear foot

10. **Work outside standard hours** - Weekend or after-hours work: $95/hour per worker premium

11. **Hazardous materials** - If any hazardous materials discovered during removal (asbestos, lead flashing, etc.): actual cost plus 15%

12. **Additional insulation** - Above R-30: $3.20/sq ft per R-value increment

13. **Snow guards** - If required: $58/linear foot

14. **Infrared survey** - If owner requires: $4,500

15. **Permits** - Building permits not included. Estimated $3,800-$5,200

16. **Testing and inspection** - Third-party testing/inspection if required by owner: actual cost plus 10%

17. **Protection of landscaping** - If special protection required beyond standard caution tape: actual cost

18. **Waste disposal fees** - If disposal fees exceed current rates due to market changes: actual cost adjustment

19. **Material price escalation** - If project start delayed beyond 60 days from bid date: material costs subject to market adjustment

20. **Substantial completion inspection** - Third-party inspection if required by owner: $2,800

21. **Phasing coordination** - If phasing extends schedule beyond 90 days: $2,500/week additional supervision

22. **Temperature monitoring** - If required by owner: actual cost of equipment plus 10%

23. **Weekend weather protection** - If weekend work not performed: $1,500/weekend for tarps and protection

24. **Multiple roof levels access** - If special equipment required for office wing roof: actual rental cost

25. **Daily cleanup** - If daily cleanup not feasible due to site conditions: weekly cleanup at no additional cost

### CLARIFICATIONS AND ASSUMPTIONS

Our bid is based on the following assumptions:

1. Existing roof can be removed without special hazardous material handling
2. Steel deck is in sound condition requiring minimal repairs
3. Wood deck in office wing is in acceptable condition
4. All existing flashings are non-hazardous materials
5. Building will be vacated or operations can continue normally during work
6. No special access restrictions beyond standard safety requirements
7. Weather conditions allow normal construction schedule
8. No union jurisdictional disputes affecting work
9. Materials can be delivered and stored on-site without special arrangements
10. Existing roof drains are functional and compatible with new system
11. Electrical power available on-site for tools and equipment
12. Phasing can be accomplished within 90-day schedule
13. Cover board not required for this building type
14. Ponding does not require correction unless causing active leaks
15. Mechanically attached system acceptable (equivalent to fully adhered for this application)
