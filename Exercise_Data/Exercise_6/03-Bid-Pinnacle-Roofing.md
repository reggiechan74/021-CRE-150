### PHASING PLAN SUMMARY

We have experience with occupied building projects and propose the following approach:

**Phase 1 (Days 1-20):** Office wing roof (5,100 sq ft)
- Isolated from main operations
- Establishes work patterns

**Phase 2 (Days 21-50):** Main roof - Section A (20,000 sq ft)
- Coordinate daily with facility manager
- End-of-day weather protection

**Phase 3 (Days 51-80):** Main roof - Section B including ponding areas (25,000 sq ft)
- Tapered insulation installed
- Final tie-ins

**Phasing Protections:**
- Maximum 10,000 sq ft exposed at any time
- Each phase made watertight by end of work day
- Weekend protection with reinforced tarps
- Coordination meetings twice weekly during critical phases

### CLARIFICATIONS AND ASSUMPTIONS

Our bid is based on the following understanding:

1. **Ponding correction:** We have included an allowance for tapered insulation. Final quantity to be confirmed during removal.

2. **Deck conditions:** We have included allowances for both steel and wood deck repairs. Based on visual inspection, these should be sufficient.

3. **Hazardous materials:** We assume minimal ACM if any (building is 1998). Testing during removal. Major abatement would be extra.

4. **Phasing:** Our schedule assumes reasonable cooperation from tenant for access and coordination. Significant phasing changes may affect schedule.

5. **HVAC coordination:** We assume HVAC shutdowns can be scheduled for weekends when needed. Weekday shutdowns require 72-hour notice.

6. **Temperature monitoring:** Owner to provide or arrange temperature monitoring in warehouse. We will coordinate around any alerts.

7. **Permits:** Building permits are typically owner responsibility. We can arrange if needed (priced separately).
