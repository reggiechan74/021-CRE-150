## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

### 1.1 Building Description
Tenebrus Capital is soliciting bids for a complete roof replacement at our industrial property located at 2550 Argentia Road, Mississauga, Ontario.

- **Building Type:** Single-story industrial warehouse with office component and mezzanine
- **Total Roof Area:** Approximately 85,000 square feet (7,900 square metres)
- **Building Dimensions:** 425 ft x 200 ft main building + 85 ft x 60 ft office wing (separate roof section)
- **Roof Type:** Existing built-up gravel roof (BUR) with modified bitumen patches - being fully replaced
- **Roof Deck:** Steel deck construction (sections of wood deck observed in office wing)
- **Building Height:** 28 feet to eave (main), 14 feet (office wing)
- **Year Built:** 1998 (main building), 2006 (office wing addition)
- **Current Roof Age:** 28 years main / 20 years office wing (original roofs)
- **Number of Roof Levels:** 3 (main roof, office wing roof, mezzanine equipment area)

### 1.2 Property Usage
- 75% warehouse/distribution (cold storage operations - temperature sensitive)
- 25% office space (occupied during construction)
- Normal business hours: Monday-Friday, 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM
- **Critical:** Warehouse houses temperature-sensitive pharmaceutical products requiring climate control

### 1.3 Known Roof Conditions
The following conditions have been identified through recent inspections:

1. **Ponding Water:** Multiple areas of standing water observed, particularly in southwest quadrant (approximately 1,200 sq ft affected). Some ponding areas exceed 48 hours after rainfall.

2. **Membrane Deterioration:** Widespread blistering and alligatoring of existing BUR membrane. Multiple previous patch repairs visible throughout.

3. **Flashing Issues:** Significant deterioration observed at parapet walls and penetration flashings. Several areas showing signs of water infiltration.

4. **Insulation Concerns:** Core cuts from 2024 assessment indicate wet insulation in approximately 15-20% of roof area. Exact extent unknown until removal.

5. **Deck Condition:** Visual inspection suggests steel deck is generally sound, but isolated corrosion observed near drains. Office wing has wood deck sections - condition unknown until existing roof removed.

6. **Previous Repairs:** Building records indicate multiple repair campaigns (2008, 2012, 2018, 2021). Complete records not available.

## 2. SCOPE OF WORK

### 2.1 Required Work
Contractor shall provide all labour, materials, equipment, and services necessary to complete the following:

1. **Complete roof removal:** Remove and legally dispose of all existing roofing materials down to the deck, including all flashings, gravel, membrane, insulation, and adhesives. **Caution:** Existing roof may contain asbestos-containing materials (ACM) or lead flashings. See Section 2.3.

2. **Deck inspection and repair:** Inspect deck for corrosion, damage, or structural issues. Steel deck repairs to be priced separately. Wood deck sections in office wing may require replacement - provide unit pricing.

3. **New roofing system installation:** Install new TPO (Thermoplastic Polyolefin) single-ply roofing system as specified in Section 3.

4. **Insulation:** Install new rigid roof insulation to meet current Ontario Building Code requirements for R-value (minimum R-30). **Note:** Wet insulation removal may exceed anticipated quantities.

5. **Flashings and penetrations:** Install new flashings at all roof edges, penetrations, HVAC curbs, skylights, and wall interfaces. Building has 47 penetrations including:
   - 12 HVAC units (various sizes)
   - 8 plumbing vents
   - 6 electrical conduits
   - 4 skylights (office wing)
   - 17 pipe penetrations
   - Multiple roof drains and overflow scuppers

6. **Drainage:** Ensure positive drainage to all roof drains. Replace any damaged drain bowls or leaders. **Address existing ponding issues** through tapered insulation or additional drains as needed.

7. **HVAC Protection:** All HVAC units must remain operational during construction. Contractor to provide temporary protection and coordinate any required shutdowns with 72-hour minimum notice.

8. **Cleanup:** Daily cleanup and final site cleanup. All debris to be removed from site daily. Building is occupied - maintain professional appearance at all times.

### 2.2 Optional Work (Priced Separately)
- Installation of snow guards (provide pricing per linear foot)
- Additional insulation above code minimum (provide pricing per R-value increment)
- Roof maintenance program (provide annual pricing)
- Infrared moisture survey of existing roof prior to removal
- Thermal imaging scan post-installation to verify installation quality
- Additional warranty coverage beyond 20 years

### 2.3 Environmental Considerations
**IMPORTANT:** Building was constructed in 1998. While asbestos use was restricted by this date, some roofing materials installed during this transition period may contain asbestos. Lead flashings are also possible.

- Bidders should assume potential for hazardous materials and include appropriate safety protocols
- If ACM is discovered during removal, work may be suspended for testing and potential abatement
- Owner will be responsible for actual abatement costs, but bidder should include contingency planning in schedule

## 3. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

### 3.1 Roofing Membrane
- **Type:** TPO (Thermoplastic Polyolefin) single-ply membrane
- **Thickness:** Minimum 60 mil
- **Colour:** White (minimum 80% solar reflectance)
- **Attachment:** Fully adhered system preferred; mechanically attached acceptable (bidders to specify approach and rationale)
- **Warranty:** Minimum 20-year manufacturer warranty on membrane
- **Seaming:** Heat-welded seams required; chemical seamers not acceptable

### 3.2 Insulation
- **Type:** Rigid polyisocyanurate board insulation
- **Minimum R-Value:** R-30 (as per Ontario Building Code 2024)
- **Configuration:** Minimum two layers with staggered joints (no single-layer applications)
- **Cover board:** Minimum 1/4 inch gypsum or high-density polyiso cover board required over all insulation
- **Tapered System:** Where required to address ponding, provide tapered insulation system (price separately if not included in base)

### 3.3 Flashings
- **Material:** Matching TPO membrane or approved metal flashings (galvanized steel or aluminum minimum; copper or premium aluminum acceptable as upgrade)
- **Height:** Minimum 8 inches above finished roof surface
- **Termination:** Properly terminated and sealed at all edges with compatible sealants
- **Reglets:** Verify existing reglets are sound; provide alternative attachment where reglets failed

### 3.4 Fasteners and Adhesives
- All fasteners and adhesives must be compatible with membrane system
- Manufacturer-approved products only
- Fastener pattern to meet wind uplift calculations for this geographic zone

### 3.5 Roof Drains and Overflow
- Verify all existing drains are compatible with new membrane
- Replace drain bowls where seal is compromised
- Ensure overflow scuppers are clear and functional
- Provide new drain clamping collars where required
